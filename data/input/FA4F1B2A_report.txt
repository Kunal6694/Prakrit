Pro-Sustainability Audit ReportEvent: Eco-Innovation Summit 2026Location: Jaipur, Rajasthan"The event achieved a 95% diversion rate from landfills by implementing a strict 'Zero Single-Use Plastic' policy. All catering was served on biodegradable areca leaf plates with stainless steel cutlery. We utilized a 10kW on-site solar array to power 100% of the audio-visual equipment and stage lighting, reducing our carbon footprint by approximately 450kg of $CO_2$. Furthermore, all leftover organic food waste (approx. 40kg) was transported to a local composting facility in Jaipur, while excess unconsumed meals were distributed to nearby shelters via our NGO partner. Digital-only ticketing and QR-code based brochures saved an estimated 15,000 sheets of paper."
